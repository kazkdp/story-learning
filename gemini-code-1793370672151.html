Talk about it: The New Broom

At the end, the witch casts a spell to make a magnificent new broom. It has:

  • Seats for the witch, cat, and dog
  • A nest for the bird
  • A shower for the clean frog

Prompt: Why did each animal get a special part on the broom? (Did it match what they like?)
